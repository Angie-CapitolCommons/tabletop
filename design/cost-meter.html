The cost meter

Four currencies, always visible, moved by every locked decision: the fast path spends goodwill, the safe path spends time, the thorough path spends money. No win threshold — it exists to make trade-offs concrete and kill the "let's do both" answer. This is the component most worth redesigning: current form is a plain gauge list. Directions worth exploring — dials, ink-well levels, a ledger strip, spend animations when a decision locks, a tension view showing the four in opposition.
